<section class="labrats__section" aria-labelledby="afterschool-how">
  <h2 id="afterschool-how">What happens in a session</h2>
  <div class="labrats__split">
    <div>
      <p>The STEM Discovery Center is a place to discover, create, build, and dive into science, with the freedom to follow your own curiosity.</p>
      <p>Each two-hour session starts with a one-hour course led by a real scientist. Then comes an hour of open maker space, set up by our staff, where kids build, test, and explore the topics that interest them most. There's a new lesson every week.</p>
      <p>The program uses Engagement Education: kids commit to their own learning, support each other as a group, and learn from scientists who care about character as much as content. <a href="{{ '/our-teaching-methods/' | relative_url }}">How we teach</a></p>
    </div>
    <dl class="labrats__day">
      <dt>Hour 1</dt><dd><strong>Teacher-led lab</strong> with a working scientist</dd>
      <dt>Hour 2</dt><dd><strong>Open maker space</strong> to build, create, and explore</dd>
    </dl>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="labrats__section labrats__split" aria-labelledby="afterschool-center">
  <div>
    <h2 id="afterschool-center">Inside the Discovery Center</h2>
    <p>Each lab starts with a short classroom lecture by one of our scientists on a specific topic, followed by a hands-on experiment. We rotate labs regularly from our library of curriculum.</p>
    <p>In open maker space, kids explore whatever they're curious about: games, experiments, and engineering challenges, using everything in the center.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h3>What's in the center</h3>
    <ul class="labrats__list">
      <li>Compound and stereo microscopes</li>
      <li>A slime station for building any kind of slime</li>
      <li>Lego robots to build and code</li>
      <li>A magnetic marble run to build your own track</li>
      <li>Messy labs: two creative labs, rotated regularly</li>
      <li>A maker space tower with materials for engineering projects, like a balloon-powered car</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</section>
