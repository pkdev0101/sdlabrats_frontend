<p>Families who attended any LabRats camp (fall or winter) or any afterschool class at the STEM Discovery Center in November or December were entered in an opportunity drawing.</p>
<p>Every class attended counted as an entry, and each day of a 3-day camp counted once. A family attending all three weeks of November and December afterschool classes plus both fall and winter camps could be entered up to 12 times. Two bikes were given away on December 1 and two on January 1.</p>
