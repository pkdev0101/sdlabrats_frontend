<div class="labrats__day-plans">
  <section>
    <h2>Grades K-3</h2>
    <h3>Life of Plants</h3>
    <p>Young horticulturalists looked at the world from a plant's point of view. By observing the plants and pollinators around them, students got an introduction to the scientific method and learned where plants came from and how they live with their environment. No green thumb required.</p>
    <h3>Creative Chemistry</h3>
    <p>From instant chemical reactions to DIY slime, a creative introduction to chemistry, the study of matter. Using safe compounds from around the house and kitchen, students explored chemical reactions and the properties used to describe matter.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <h2>Grades 4-8</h2>
    <h3>Secret Agent Science</h3>
    <p>Every spy needs a gadget person. Students learned techniques from chemistry to computer science: sending secret messages with chemistry, coding messages only they could read, and using those skills to solve a treasure hunt.</p>
    <h3>Forensic Science</h3>
    <p>It's like a crime show, but without the crime. Students learned how scientists examine a "crime" scene and find useful evidence with fingerprinting and chemical analysis.</p>
  </section>
