<p>Ever wondered what happens when real science meets hands-on tinkering? Bring your family to see the STEM Discovery Center. Whether your child is an aspiring engineer, a budding biologist, or just loves to build and test things, the center is built to spark imagination and critical thinking.</p>

<h3>During your one-hour tour</h3>
<ul class="labrats__list">
  <li>A guided walk through our discovery spaces with our scientists and educators</li>
  <li>Demos of the makerspace tools, build stations, and creative projects</li>
  <li>Mini experiments, engineering challenges, and hands-on science stations for kids</li>
  <li>Early access to upcoming class schedules, special event discounts, and Q&amp;A with our leadership team</li>
</ul>

<h3>Why timed entry?</h3>
<p>Tours run in one-hour blocks so every family gets real time at the workbenches without crowding. Space in each block is limited. Choose your day and time in the registration form.</p>

<dl class="labrats__day">
  <dt>Cost</dt><dd>Free for families</dd>
  <dt>Best for</dt><dd>Kids in grades K-8 and their parents or guardians</dd>
  <dt>Length</dt><dd>60 minutes, including the guided tour and hands-on time</dd>
  <dt>Parking</dt><dd>Free on-site parking</dd>
</dl>
