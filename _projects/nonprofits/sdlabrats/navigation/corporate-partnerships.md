    <h2 id="benefits-title">What sponsors get</h2>
    <h3>Financial incentives</h3>
    <p>Sponsorship can bring tax incentives; many companies sponsor and donate partly because of the corporate tax breaks available. A sponsorship relationship with LabRats can also lead to other incentives and eligibility for grants and awards.</p>
    <h3>Brand awareness</h3>
    <p>Sponsorship raises your company's profile locally, regionally, and nationally. Customers connect your name with a specific cause, STEAM education for kids, and your brand reaches audiences outside your usual market. It also furthers your corporate social responsibility.</p>
    <h3>Corporate reputation</h3>
    <p>Sponsorship shapes how employees, customers, and other organizations see a company. Our partnerships tie your organization to philanthropy, so people associate your brand with social good and with the cause itself. The previous site cited a figure that more than 75% of millennial employees prefer to work for companies active in charitable giving.</p>
