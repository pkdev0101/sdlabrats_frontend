Why families pick LabRats camps

For parents

The curriculum is written by working scientists and engineers who know how to make hard ideas approachable. Camps build on the NGSS topics kids cover in public and charter school. Along the way, kids practice:

  • Spatial reasoning and design
  • Sticking with a problem when the first try fails
  • Confidence that they can build a working solution

For kids

Campers get to design their own solutions and see their ideas come to life. They love:

  • Making a mess in hands-on labs
  • Solving challenges in their own way
  • Free time in the maker space to chase their own projects
