      <h2>Dr. Shawn Carlson</h2>
      <p class="labrats__role">Chief Science Officer</p>
      <p>Shawn Carlson ("Dr. Shawn") earned bachelor's degrees in physics and applied mathematics at UC Berkeley in 1981, then a master's in physics in 1983 and a Ph.D. in nuclear physics in 1989 at UCLA. He left academic research in 1993 to find new and effective ways to inspire young people to love learning STEM, and that work won him a MacArthur Fellowship in 1999. He's a nationally known science writer and the creative mind behind LabRats.</p>
      <p>In 2008 he codified what he had learned about turning students into passionate lifelong learners into a system of instruction called Engagement Education. It works in three spheres. First, it inspires students to commit to their own education. Second, it creates learning communities where students naturally support each other. Third, it builds strong bonds between students and teachers by focusing on character as well as academics. LabRats teaches STEM with this approach. <a href="{{ '/our-teaching-methods/' | relative_url }}">More on our teaching methods</a></p>

      <h2>J. Ryan Merrill</h2>
      <p class="labrats__role">Vice President and Director of Education</p>
      <p>Ryan earned his master's degree in chemistry at the University of Colorado Boulder in 2015, researching organic polymers under Professor Christopher Bowman. He earned his bachelor's in chemistry at UC Santa Barbara in 2013, where he spent more than three years researching mass spectrometry and the proteins involved in type 2 diabetes under Professor Michael Bowers.</p>
      <p>He taught general chemistry classes and labs at the University of Colorado for two years and has tutored STEM classes for more than six years. Ryan grew up in Encinitas and attended LCCHS. He loves the local community and wants to give back by sharing his love of science. At LabRats he develops curriculum, teaches labs, and runs demonstrations and experiments for local kids.</p>
