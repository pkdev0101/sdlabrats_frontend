<p>A summer camp about the science of the human senses. Your senses are like built-in scientific instruments, and campers explore how their bodies detect and interpret the world around them.</p>
<p>Kids also dig into the science of balance: not just how we stay upright, but how balance matters in engineering everything from medicine coolers to chemical processes.</p>
