    <p>The festival reports more than 1,000 attendees, $1.2 million raised since it began, and 60 food and wine vendors.</p>
    <p>All proceeds were divided among nonprofits in the children's and community categories. Attendees had to be 21 or older. All beneficiaries were 501(c)(3) charities or 501(c)(6) nonprofits, and buyers could deduct their ticket price minus $25. Choosing SDLabRats at checkout sent ticket funds to future STEM education.</p>
