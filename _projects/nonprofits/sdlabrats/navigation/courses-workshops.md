<section class="labrats__section" aria-labelledby="courses-approach">
  <h2 id="courses-approach">Engagement Education</h2>
  <p>Our approach is called Engagement Education: scientists teaching science in ways that are fun and engaging. It helps students build character, self-confidence, and a real passion for what they learn. The goal is an encouraging STEAM education that helps every kid find their passion and do their best.</p>
</section>

<section class="labrats__section labrats__split" aria-labelledby="courses-types">
  <div>
    <h2 id="courses-types">Enrichment courses</h2>
    <p>Multi-week courses for three age bands: K-2, 3-5, and 6-8. We fit the curriculum to each school, with hands-on labs of an hour or more to suit your students' schedule.</p>
    <p>Topics range from NGSS physical and life science, like electricity, magnetism, DNA, and digestion, to subjects kids will need for the future: coding, robotics, and forensics.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h2>Workshops</h2>
    <p>One-time sessions using our Mobile Lab, which can deliver any of our 100+ hands-on lessons to your classroom or event. We can also help scout troops earn the STEM merit badge.</p>
    <p>Every lab gets students running experiments with their own hands, with simple demonstrations of advanced ideas.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="labrats__section" aria-labelledby="courses-educators">
  <h2 id="courses-educators">Who teaches</h2>
  <p>Courses are taught by working scientists, including Dr. Shawn Carlson, a nuclear physicist and nationally known science writer, and J. Ryan Merrill, a chemist who taught chemistry classes and labs at the University of Colorado Boulder.</p>
