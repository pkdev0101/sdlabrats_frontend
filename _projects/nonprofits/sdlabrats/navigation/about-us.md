  <h2 id="who-title">Who we are</h2>
  <p class="labrats__motto">To make advanced STEM learning accessible, engaging, and exciting for all kids, no matter their zip code.</p>
  <div class="labrats__split">
    <div>
      <p>San Diego LabRats is a {{ org.nonprofit }} where working scientists teach hands-on science to kids in grades K-8. Our teachers love their fields and love teaching the next generation; we use hands-on lab curriculum and current techniques, and we bring the fun back to learning science.</p>
      <p>Our goal is to offer the program to underserved kids throughout San Diego County, which is only possible with our community's support.</p>
      <p>Our home is the STEM Discovery Center at {{ org.address.street }}, {{ org.address.city }}, {{ org.address.region }} {{ org.address.postal }}, and our Mobile STEAM Lab visits schools and events across the county. <a href="{{ org.address.map_url }}" rel="noopener">Get directions</a></p>

<section class="labrats__section" id="philosophy" aria-labelledby="philosophy-title">
  <h2 id="philosophy-title">How we teach</h2>
  <div class="labrats__split">
    <div>
      <p>Kids learn best by doing, especially when they chase their own ideas with someone more knowledgeable nearby. Project-based learning builds critical thinking, problem solving, teamwork, and self-management.</p>
      <p>Our teachers are scientists who have done their own research. They turn textbook ideas into hands-on labs tied to the real world, showing students that science built the modern marvels around them. Everything we do builds a growth mindset: labs push kids to think problems through rather than follow directions, and when a student asks "what if," we say "let's try it." Kids learn that not understanding something at first doesn't mean they never will, and they gain the confidence to keep going instead of shutting down. Like sports teams and scouting, classes build community through teamwork and friendly competition: dynamic and fun, not "sit and listen."</p>
      <p>An education in science and technology is nothing less than the power to transform the world with a single idea. The modern world was built by hundreds of thousands of people who understood how nature works and solved practical problems; a few made incredible contributions, most made modest ones, and every one of them knew they had made a difference.</p>
      <p>Read more: <a href="{{ '/why-learn-science/' | relative_url }}">Why learn science?</a> and <a href="{{ '/our-teaching-methods/' | relative_url }}">Our teaching methods</a></p>
    </div>
    <figure class="labrats__figure">
      <img src="{{ '/images/projects/sdlabrats/static-hair.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="A grinning student's hair stands on end from static electricity" width="750" height="500" loading="lazy">
    </figure>
  </div>
  <div class="labrats__split">
    <div>
      <h3>The LabRats Code of Honor</h3>
      <ol class="labrats__honor">
        <li>Be fair, kind, and generous to all</li>
        <li>Respect yourself and all others</li>
        <li>Never cheat or accept the cheating of others</li>
        <li>Think like a scientist</li>
        <li>Always do the smart thing</li>
        <li>Always follow through</li>
        <li>Work for a higher purpose</li>
      </ol>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h3>The LabRats motto</h3>
      <p class="labrats__motto">Investigate. Do the experiment!</p>
      <p>Students don't have to rely on what someone else tells them is true. They have the power to discover the truth for themselves, and we encourage them to use it every day.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
