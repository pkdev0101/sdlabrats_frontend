    <h2 id="project-title">Project-based learning</h2>
    <p>Project-based learning is critical to student success. It builds critical thinking, problem solving, teamwork, and self-management.</p>
    <h3>Why learn science?</h3>
    <p>An education in science and technology is nothing less than the power to transform the world with a single idea. Our exercises show students that the modern world was built by hundreds of thousands of people who understood how nature works and were good at solving practical problems. A few made incredible contributions; most made modest ones. You may not know their names, but every one of them knew they had made a difference.</p>

  <h2 id="methods-title">Scientists as mentors</h2>
  <div class="labrats__split">
    <div>
      <p>Students need room to explore, ask questions, and be challenged, plus a knowledgeable mentor to guide them. Our teachers are scientists who have done their own research and love passing that expertise to the next generation.</p>
      <p>We take ideas students would normally meet only in textbooks and turn them into hands-on labs and demos tied to the real world, which shows students that science built the modern marvels around them.</p>
    </div>
    <div>
      <p>Everything we do is meant to build a growth mindset. Our labs push students to think problems through rather than follow directions. When a student asks "what if," we say "let's try it." They learn that not understanding something at first doesn't mean they never will, and they gain the confidence to keep going instead of shutting down.</p>
      <p>Like sports teams and scouting, our classes build community, with teamwork and friendly competition in the lesson plans. The result is a classroom that's dynamic and fun, not "sit and listen."</p>
    </div>
  </div>

<section class="labrats__section labrats__band" aria-labelledby="honor-title">
  <h2 id="honor-title">The LabRats Code of Honor</h2>
  <ol class="labrats__honor">
    <li>Be fair, kind, and generous to all</li>
    <li>Respect yourself and all others</li>
    <li>Never cheat or accept the cheating of others</li>
    <li>Think like a scientist</li>
    <li>Always do the smart thing</li>
    <li>Always follow through</li>
    <li>Work for a higher purpose</li>
  </ol>
</section>

<section class="labrats__section" aria-labelledby="motto-title">
  <h2 id="motto-title">The LabRats motto</h2>
  <p class="labrats__motto">Investigate. Do the experiment!</p>
  <p>Students don't have to rely on what someone else tells them is true. They have the power to discover the truth for themselves, and we encourage them to use it every day.</p>
