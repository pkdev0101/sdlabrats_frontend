<p>It's like a crime show, but without the crime. At Forensic Science Camp, kids become scientific sleuths and learn how real forensic scientists use evidence to solve mysteries. Your child will:</p>
<ul class="labrats__list">
  <li>Collect and analyze fingerprints using professional techniques</li>
  <li>Examine mysterious substances with chemical analysis</li>
  <li>Decipher secret messages with forensic tools</li>
  <li>Work with teammates to solve mysteries</li>
</ul>
<p>The curriculum is written by real scientists who know how to make forensic concepts engaging and age-appropriate. Along the way, kids build critical thinking, scientific reasoning, and attention to detail that help them in school and beyond.</p>
