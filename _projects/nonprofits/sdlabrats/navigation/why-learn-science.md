  <div>
    <h2 id="what-title">What is science?</h2>
    <p class="labrats__motto">Science is everything we know about the physical universe, and how we know it.</p>
    <p>That restates the "systemized body of knowledge" definition usually taught in school, and it makes clear that science is both the facts and the process used to discover them.</p>

  <div>
    <h2 id="doing-title">What is doing science about?</h2>
    <p class="labrats__motto">Making sure you're not fooling yourself.</p>
    <p>That is the heart of the scientific enterprise: each scientist working painstakingly to avoid fooling themselves. If you don't know how to check whether you're fooling yourself, you're more likely to be fooled. We teach students that reasoned self-skepticism gives them a big advantage in every part of life.</p>
    <p>Science isn't just one way to understand nature. It's the best approach we have to understanding the physical universe.</p>

<section class="labrats__section labrats__split" aria-labelledby="scientist-title">
  <div>
    <h2 id="scientist-title">What makes a scientist a scientist?</h2>
    <p class="labrats__motto">Always fit theories to facts, never facts to theories.</p>
    <p>We all have beliefs. Many people, faced with facts that contradict a belief, bend their reading of the facts to fit what they want to believe. A scientist makes a personal commitment to a worldview where the truth matters more than any belief they hold right now.</p>
    <p>A scientist stays skeptical of their own ideas and is willing to change them when credible evidence shows they're wrong.</p>
