<div class="labrats__split">
  <div>
    <p>The STEAM Discovery Center was open Fridays from 1 to 4 pm at the Encinitas Community Center, 1140 Oakcrest Park Dr, Encinitas, CA 92024. Each day a real scientist led a one-hour course, with open maker space time before and after. Full scholarships were provided by TING Internet. One day cost $50; all four weeks cost $185.</p>
    <p>The program used three forms of Engagement Education: self, social, and custodial engagement.</p>

<div class="labrats__day-plans">
  <section>
    <h2>Grades K-3: Robotics and maker space</h2>
    <dl class="labrats__day">
      <dt>1:00–2:00 pm</dt><dd>Open maker space</dd>
      <dt>2:00–3:00 pm</dt><dd>Teacher-led course: Robotics, Animal Style (room 120)</dd>
      <dt>3:00–4:00 pm</dt><dd>Open maker space</dd>
    </dl>
    <p>Robots in Space taught students to build and code robots that solve challenges from space travel. Maker space topics changed daily: magnetism, engineering, catapults, airplanes, and rockets.</p>
  </section>
  <section>
    <h2>Grades 4-8: Forensics and maker space</h2>
    <dl class="labrats__day">
      <dt>1:00–2:00 pm</dt><dd>Teacher-led course: Forensic Science (room 140)</dd>
      <dt>2:00–4:00 pm</dt><dd>Open maker space</dd>
    </dl>
    <p>Secret Agent Science used chemistry, physics, and computer science to solve spy challenges. Maker space topics included building and coding robots and building launchers like catapults and air cannons.</p>
  </section>
